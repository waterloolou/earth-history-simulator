Earth History Simulator

Scrub from the formation of Earth 4.5 billion years ago down to individual days of recorded history, on one zoomable timeline.

Controls

  • Scroll or + / − — zoom the timeline (zooms toward the cursor/playhead)
  • Drag the timeline — scrub through time
  • Shift-drag (or right-drag) the timeline — pan without moving the playhead
  • Double-click a period on the timeline — drill into just that span
  • Drag the globe — rotate it (releases with inertia)
  • Space — pause/resume playback
  • ← / → — slow down / speed up playback
  • W — toggle between the globe and the map
  • R — reset to the full 4.5-billion-year view

Finding things

Use the search box (top right) to jump straight to any historical, scientific, cultural, or biographical event. The Share button copies a link back to exactly what you're looking at.
